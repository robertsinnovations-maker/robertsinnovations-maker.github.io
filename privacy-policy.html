RIFT Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

RIFT is a tabletop game companion application being developed by Roberts Innovations LLC.

1. Current Development Status

RIFT is currently under development and has not been publicly released. Public user accounts, advertising, and other planned online features are not currently operating in a released app.

2. Information We Collect

RIFT does not currently collect personal information from public app users because the application has not been publicly released.

Future versions may process information needed for accounts, saved game data, tournament participation, application functionality, advertising, and user-selected features. This policy will be updated to describe actual data practices before those features are launched.

3. Advertising

RIFT may offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob in a future release.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, and approximate location derived from technical signals, subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

Learn more about Google's data practices at Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Privacy Choices

Where required, RIFT intends to provide advertising consent and privacy choices, including options to decline consent or manage preferences.

These controls will be configured and reviewed before applicable advertising functionality becomes available.

5. Tournament and Game Features

Future RIFT features may include game records, card collections, tournament management, and virtual in-game rewards.

Any collection, use, sharing, retention, and deletion of related information will be disclosed when these features are implemented.

6. Children's Privacy

RIFT's intended audience and applicable age-related protections will be determined before public release. Any features involving advertising or personal information will require appropriate safeguards and disclosures.

7. Data Security and Retention

Before launching features that process personal information, Roberts Innovations LLC intends to establish appropriate safeguards, retention practices, and procedures for handling user requests.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as RIFT develops. Material changes to data collection or advertising practices will be reflected in an updated version of this page.
